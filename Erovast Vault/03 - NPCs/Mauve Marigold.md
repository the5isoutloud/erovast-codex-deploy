# Mauve Marigold

![[Mauve_Marigold.png]]

## Summary

Mauve Marigold is the person [[Duke Tristan Blackwood]] sent the party to find in [[Havenport]]. They are to stay with her until he sends word. **They met her this session at the Salt House.** Source: [[2026.09.22|2026.09.22]], [[2026.09.29|2026.09.29]].

## Notes

- The party met Mauve at the Salt House, the survivors' community center in Havenport's dock ward. She was in charge: a middle-aged woman wearing a weathered sailor's pin that marks her authority.
- She recognized [[Cassian]] as **Cassian Fortescue** at once and addressed him by name.
- She has known [[Duke Tristan Blackwood]] since before he held his current title as Duke. She said Tristan doesn't send people this far west by accident. *"Which is it — are you traders, or are you here because Tristan thinks Havenport needs something from you, or he needs something from me?"*
- She asked pointedly whether the party is here because of trade or because of Tristan's agenda.
- She explained Havenport's decline: when [[House Westermere]] fell 15 years ago, the town lost ships, merchants, trade, economy and infrastructure. *"Lately people aren't only leaving — they're dying."* She believes what happened at the manor 15 years ago has everything to do with what her people face now: the entire Westermere family went missing in an instant, a black wall of force blocked the undercroft, and the Wither began creeping in over the following years. At first two or three individuals a year were taken; in the last five or six years, the town fell apart. Mauve was 31 when it happened and knew some of the people who *"brought something into that manor that they couldn't control."*
- She described the Wither's progression: people get tired and sick, stop eating, stop talking, and *"it's like watching somebody disappear before their body's gotten the message. Black veins begin to crawl beneath their skin."*
- She introduced the party to **[[Hesta Cole]]**, Havenport's closest thing to a nurse — a practical healer who tends Wither victims but has reached her limits.
- She agreed to let the party stay at the Salt House for a short period. She asked them to pay a fair price for a bed.
- When [[Aldric]] introduced himself as **Aldrick Havenport**, she was puzzled: *"Havenport is not a surname."* He shrugged it off.
- She said she's known [[Ian]] (Ser Ian Wren) for a long time and finds him charming, but he doesn't visit the Salt House.
- She cautioned the party against spending too much time in Westermere Manor: *"It is exceedingly dangerous and unfortunately I don't know if I can help your friend."* She said she doesn't know what game Blackwood is playing by sending them to her.
- She said the more people who have perished to the Wither, the more ghosts seem to gather around the manor. *"There's some clue buried beneath that place but none of us dare brave it to find out."*
- She asked the party, if they return to the manor, to find out why Havenport's dead aren't staying dead.
- She is a survivor of Havenport's decline, part of "Mav and her people" who took refuge in the village from the undead below the manor.

Source: [[2026.09.22|2026.09.22]], [[2026.09.29|2026.09.29]].
