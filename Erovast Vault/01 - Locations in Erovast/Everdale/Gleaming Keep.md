# Gleaming Keep

## Summary

The Gleaming Keep crowns the hill above [[Everdale]]. According to local lore, King Aldrich and his powerful mages transmuted the keep's stonework into metal so it would endure, causing it to gleam in the light. Source: [[2026.05.05|2026.05.05]].

## Related Lore

- The keep houses or protects the [[Empty Throne]], which has remained unfilled since King Aldrich.
- The [[Oathkeepers]] guard the keep and preserve it for the hoped-for return of King Aldrich.
- The [[King's Tourney]] takes place in the valley below the keep.

## Inside the Keep

The party first entered on [[2026.09.22|2026.09.22]]. Oathkeepers guard its open metal doors and check visitors against a list. The walls were once pristine stonework, perhaps by master masons, dwarves or elves, and King Aldrich's archmages transmuted them into near-impenetrable steel. The halls hold ornate pews, banners and candelabra, but they are vast and empty, kept only by "bannerless knights" protecting the place from the noble houses. A chamber with a high porthole holds a **teleportation circle** tended by six Oathkeeper knights and a robed acolyte. It runs on spell draught and connects to other surviving circles from King Aldrich's era, including one in [[Havenport]]. See [[Oathkeepers]]. Source: [[2026.09.22|2026.09.22]].
