# Renny Blackwood

## Summary

Renny Blackwood is **[[Duke Tristan Blackwood]]'s nephew and manservant**. Scrawny, red-faced, and sweaty from carrying his uncle's reward pouch, with white hair and the look of a man in his late 60s. Source: [[00 - Game Log/2026.08.11|2026.08.11]].

## Notes

- Delivered the party's [[Crown Gauntlet]] victory reward from Duke Blackwood: roughly 1,500 gold split among the party plus individualized magic items (see [[Compete in the King's Tourney]]).
- Relayed Duke Blackwood's invitation to visit his tent that evening "to discuss business"—not yet acted on by the party.
- Politely declined [[Cassian]]'s offer of a drink, citing "other matters to attend," and left the empty reward pouch with the party at Cassian's request before departing.
- On [[00 - Game Log/2026.08.18|2026.08.18]], the party set out to find him again after [[Aldrich]] was driven to exhaustion level 6 by the King's Cup drinking tournament, hoping he'd know of a cleric who could help before the Menagerie bout—not yet resolved on-page.
- Granted the party an emergency audience with Duke Blackwood after they told him they had information he was looking for. Left a short good-luck note for the party at [[Holt]]'s check-in desk ahead of their Menagerie bout: "shorthanded on such short notice... best of luck don't die." Source: [[00 - Game Log/2026.08.25|2026.08.25]].
- Rushed into the [[Ragged Flagon]] breathless and panicked after the manticore bout to summon the party urgently to Duke Blackwood's tent. Source: [[00 - Game Log/2026.09.15|2026.09.15]].

## Relationships

- [[Duke Tristan Blackwood]]: His uncle and employer.
- [[Cassian]]: Made light small talk with him; found him a good sport.
