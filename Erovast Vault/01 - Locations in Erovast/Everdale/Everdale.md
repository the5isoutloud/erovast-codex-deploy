# Everdale

## Summary

Everdale is the human capital city and the host of the annual [[King's Tourney]]. Roads converge here toward a hill crowned by the [[Gleaming Keep]], with the tournament grounds and [[King's Tourney]] spreading through the valley below. Source: [[00 - Game Log/2026.05.05|2026.05.05]].

## Notable Features

- **[[Gleaming Keep]]:** a keep built from stone supposedly transmuted into metal by King Aldrich's mages. It sits on a cliff above the city and reflects the light, giving it its name. Source: [[00 - Game Log/2026.05.05|2026.05.05]].
- **[[Empty Throne]]:** the throne once held by King Aldrich. It has not been filled for centuries. Source: [[00 - Game Log/2026.05.05|2026.05.05]].
- **[[King's Tourney]]:** a two-week annual tournament that brings Everdale to life and sustains much of the local economy. Source: [[00 - Game Log/2026.05.05|2026.05.05]].
- **[[Stone Sanctum]]:** an old moss-and-vine-covered cathedral in the hamlet above the tourney grounds, with an attached graveyard. Source: [[00 - Game Log/2026.05.12|2026.05.12]].
- **[[Ragged Flagon]]:** a makeshift tavern in the King's Tourney campgrounds where nobles don't typically frequent. Source: [[00 - Game Log/2026.05.12|2026.05.12]].

## Governance

Everdale is protected and administered by the [[Oathkeepers]], an order formed from three knightly orders. They protect the keep, guard the Empty Throne, police the tournament, and help oversee the local economy and settlement. They believe King Aldrich will someday return. Source: [[00 - Game Log/2026.05.05|2026.05.05]].

## Noble Houses

- [[House Godrin]]: a wealthy noble house known for mana crystals, artifacts, arcane power, and a ruthless belief in the right to rule. [[Cassian]] recognized their white armor and banner at the tourney grounds. Source: [[00 - Game Log/2026.05.05|2026.05.05]].
- [[House Wulfhelm]]: one of the most powerful houses in the Crownlands. They won last year's King's Tourney and currently hold the title and deed to the [[Thornwood Vale]]. Source: [[00 - Game Log/2026.05.05|2026.05.05]].

## Party Visit

[[Jevon]] guided [[Odine Dunmere]], [[Cassian]], [[Aldrich]], and [[Azrith]] into the city for the King's Tourney, then disappeared into the crowd. The party registered for the Proving Grounds as **Cassian's Crushing Crusaders**. They later discovered Jevon had been taken by [[House Godrin]] soldiers and found him tied up in the [[Stone Sanctum]] graveyard. After rescuing him and defeating a possessed knight, the party learned that House Godrin was interrogating Jevon about his teacher, a witch named [[Maralynn]] from the [[Thornwood Vale]]. See [[Rescue Jevon]] and [[Investigate the Pestilence]]. Source: [[00 - Game Log/2026.05.05|2026.05.05]], [[00 - Game Log/2026.05.12|2026.05.12]].
