## Overview

A half-elf sorceress named [[Odine Dunmere]] leaves her isolated elven homeland, the [[Thornwood Vale]], because a creeping corruption, later called **the Wither**, is killing its crops, its creatures and its people. She joins three companions on the road: the silver-tongued noble [[Cassian]], the horned, ghost-chasing [[Azrith]], and the tiefling ex-guard [[Aldric]]. Together they arrive in [[Everdale]], seat of the long-vacant [[Empty Throne]], during the annual [[King's Tourney]]. The tourney's grand prize is the **deed to the Vale itself**, which the great noble houses have fought over for a decade. The four enter as _Cassian's Crushing Crusaders_, an unknown team of underdogs. With the backing of the scheming [[Duke Tristan Blackwood]], they climb from the muddy Proving Grounds to the famed [[Crown Gauntlet]], which no team had cleared in seven years. Along the way they win the hearts of the common folk and the attention of the nobility.

The party is pulled into older and darker currents. The arcane [[House Godrin]] has been hunting the young mage [[Jevon]] to reach his teacher, [[Maralynn]], a legendary witch of the Vale they have now confirmed is alive. The dwarf smith [[Gruvelda Duskbelt]] reveals that Odine's grandmother, Mara, gave her life to hold back the entity behind the Wither, and that humanity's tale of its King's victory over it is a lie. Cassian carries a bargain with [[The Masked Figure]] who visits his dreams. The Wither is spreading into the Crownlands. When the party freed a captive manticore in [[The Menagerie]] instead of slaying it, they scandalized the tourney. Blackwood sent them by ancient teleportation circle to [[Havenport]], a ruined seaport that turns out to be Aldric's forgotten home. There they heard the Oathkeepers' legend of the **Dark King**, which echoes Odine's family stories. Then Azrith leapt headlong through a curtain into the realm of death, and returned with news that **[[Aldric]] has a mother — Eleanor Gray — who named him after King Aldrich and always meant to bring him home.** The party also learned that **[[House Wulfhelm]] killed [[House Westermere]]** in a coup 15 years ago to silence a secret, and that a putrid spirit commanding the undead waits beyond the black veil in the Westermere undercroft. No one in Havenport has managed to break the veil, and passing through it demands a toll of the party's own life force. They paid it, passed beyond, and **destroyed the Warden of Death** — a divine warden that [[House Wulfhelm]] had twisted into a magnet for Havenport's souls, and the very mechanism of their trap. With the Warden gone, Havenport's dead were freed and the town pledged its support to the party — and the Crusaders returned to Everdale to find the tourney militarized and ten days from a final contest that someone, Blackwood believes, has already decided the outcome of.

## Timeline

### Prologue: The Road to Everdale

_Before the first logged session._ Odine, Cassian, Aldric and Azrith travel toward Everdale with [[Jevon]], a young mage carrying a glowing elven box. On the road they meet Sir Televast, an [[Oathkeepers|Oathkeeper]] knight, and encounter a horse twisted by the same black corruption Odine has seen in the Vale. It is her first sign that the curse has escaped her homeland. See [[2026.05.05]].

### Arc I: Arrival at the Tourney

**[[2026.05.05]] "The King's Fair"**
The party reaches Everdale, with the [[Gleaming Keep]] shining above a sprawling festival. Cassian glimpses the black, tentacle-like marks on Odine's arm. The party registers for the Proving Grounds and meets the tourney's colorful locals: the registrar [[Holt]], the cook [[Slug]], the brewer [[Minnie]], the apothecary [[Marea Voss]] and the bard [[Virellan Le'Strange]]. They also catch sight of [[Kardis Wulfhelm]], prince of the house that holds the Vale's deed. Then Jevon vanishes, taken by white-armored knights, and Azrith finds his box dropped in the mud.

**[[2026.05.12]] "The Stone Sanctum"**
The party pitches itself as "ghost hunters" to the dwarven smith [[Gruvelda Duskbelt]]. She shows Odine a severed hand bearing the same corruption; she cut off her own to survive it. Following rumors of nobles meddling in a hilltop graveyard, the party finds Jevon bound at the [[Stone Sanctum]] and destroys the ghost-possessed [[House Godrin]] soldier guarding him. Jevon reveals that Godrin wanted his teacher, a witch of the Vale named [[Maralynn]]. He goes into hiding and gives Odine a sending stone.

**[[2026.05.19]] "A Deal in Dreams"**
At the commoners' tavern, the [[Ragged Flagon]], run by the orc [[Rory]], the party convinces Gruvelda that the ghost job is done. She arms Aldric with a Sentinel Shield and points them to her brother, [[Grimhall Duskbelt]]. Cassian talks the party into a borrowed tent on the noble hill of [[Boarhead Tor]]. That night, [[The Masked Figure]] visits his dreams and strikes a bargain: keep Odine close and stir trouble among the houses, and the Figure will clear the tourney's champions from his path and reveal where his family's strange dice came from.

### Arc II: The Proving Grounds

**[[2026.06.02]] "First Blood in the Mud"**
In their first bout, Cassian and Azrith are both knocked out. Aldric holds the line alone until Odine revives them, and the Crusaders win to a roaring crowd. The minor lord [[Duke Tristan Blackwood]] takes notice. Cassian's side bet is lost on a technicality.

**[[2026.06.11]] "The Straw Doll"**
Blackwood sponsors the party for 8% of any winnings, paying a 750 gp advance and magic items collected at [[Clover Hill]]. Cassian is publicly named "Lord Fortescue," heir of [[House Fortescue]]. Odine finds a century-old straw doll from the Vale, which unlocks Gruvelda's trust. The smith reveals that she knew Odine's grandmother Mara, who **sacrificed herself** to cast the entity back. She names Odine one of **the Touched**. And she confirms that the King's "victory" over the blight 150 years ago was a lie. Gruvelda gifts Odine an Elven Chain and Cassian the mysterious Chain of the Unbroken.

**[[2026.06.23]] "The Crown Gauntlet, Part One"**
Jevon slips into camp at night; he is still hunted. Cassian learns that House Godrin wins the tourney to mine **mana crystals** in the Vale. The party bets 50 gp on themselves at 3× odds, hypes the crowd, and begins the [[Crown Gauntlet]], a 40-foot obstacle tower over a mud pit. Illusions and laughter spells neutralize most of the guards before the clock is paused.

**[[2026.07.14]] "Six Seconds Left"**
The party maxes out the crowd's cheers, scales the wall, frees a jammed gear-tower with Cassian's chain, and hauls Odine up in a last-second rescue. All four ring the bell with six seconds to spare, the first team to finish in seven years. Blackwood offers only a slow, reserved clap.

### Arc III: Shadows of House Godrin

**Untranscribed session "The Wither"**
_Known only from the GM's recap (see [[2026.08.11]])._ The party buys its own campsite, learns the blight's true name, **the Wither**, from a disillusioned Oathkeeper, and rescues Jevon from Godrin's knights a second time. They learn that Godrin wants to know **who gave Jevon his stone**.

**[[2026.08.11]] "Spoils and Ledgers"**
Blackwood's nephew [[Renny Blackwood]] delivers the Gauntlet prize: about 1,500 gp and a magic item for each hero. The party sets up a hidden dead-drop to keep Jevon supplied. Gruvelda quietly reveals that House Godrin keeps ledgers of its dealings. Holt confirms the Crusaders have been promoted to [[The Menagerie]], the tourney's beast arena. At the table, the GM corrects a running mix-up: the knights hunting Jevon were always **House Godrin**, not [[House Oswall]].

**[[2026.08.18]] "The King's Cup"**
Blackwood finally explains the true stakes. Godrin and [[House Wulfhelm]] have traded the Vale for a decade. [[House Corwyn]] believes the Vale hides a secret that could topple them both. Godrin has been abducting hedge mages and, rumor says, children. He hands Cassian a sealed intelligence note on Godrin's camp, [[White Feather Tor]]. That night Aldric enters Rory's drinking contest, the King's Cup, and wins it. His last rival, a dwarf, dies at the table, and Aldric is left incapacitated at exhaustion level 6.

**[[2026.08.25]] "She's Alive"**
The priest [[Father Caldus Vey]] nurses Aldric back. Cassian and Azrith infiltrate White Feather Tor invisibly. They steal a Godrin letter and overhear [[Percival Godrin]]: Maralynn **is alive, and they have found her**. They also glimpse Godrin's towering champion, [[Sir Thorin Cassavar]], "the Crow." To protect Maralynn, Odine invents a decoy witch, "Aerendyl Morath," and Blackwood agrees to spread the lie. That evening the party walks into the Menagerie to face a manticore.

### Arc IV: The Menagerie

**[[2026.09.08]] "Unchained"**
Mid-battle, the manticore speaks, and Cassian chooses words over steel. He learns that the beast is a captive held by a trebuchet-and-net failsafe and talks it down. He then rides it to destroy the machine and sets it free. [[The Freed Manticore]] flies off promising to visit. The paying crowd boos and the judges are in uproar, while peasants on the cliffs cheer. Blackwood slips out of his box without a word.

**[[2026.09.15]] "That Was Genius"**
The party flees in a haze of burning banners. Holt warns that the stunt may end their tourney run, while the common folk hail them as folk heroes. In private, Blackwood calls it _"fucking genius."_ The scandal is the perfect distraction for his own schemes. But he warns that mercy will one day cost lives. He sends them away to lie low under his stern captain, [[Captain Garran]], to a town called [[Havenport]]. That is also Aldric's surname. The party reaches level 5.

### Arc V: Havenport

**[[2026.09.22]] "Beyond the Curtain"**
Blackwood's gold buys passage through an ancient **teleportation circle** in the Gleaming Keep. It is powered by **spell draught**, a liquid made from the Vale's mana crystals. The party arrives at [[Westermere Manor]] in rain-lashed, dying Havenport. There they learn it is **Aldric's hometown**. The lone Oathkeeper [[Ian]] tells them the legend of the **Dark King**, a corrupting power from a far realm. King Aldrich defeated it alone, and all that was found of him afterward was a statue of himself in the Vale. Odine has seen that statue with her own eyes. In the haunted undercroft, Azrith leaps into **the Curtain**, a gate to the realm of death, and vanishes. The ghost [[Samantha Westermere]] explains what it is, and the party studies a black veil of pure entropy that is sealing off the depths.

**[[2026.09.29]] "Aldrick of Havenport"** (session 15)
With Azrith's player back at the table, the party found [[Mauve Marigold]] at the Salt House and learned the full story of Havenport's decline: [[House Westermere]] fell 15 years ago when [[House Wulfhelm]] staged a coup to silence a secret, and the Wither crept in afterward, taking victims one by one until the town collapsed. [[Odine Dunmere|Odine]] confirmed the same 15-year timeline for the Wither in the Vale. [[Cassian]] revealed his full title, Lord Fortescue, to Mauve. Meanwhile, [[Azrith]] fought through the ethereal plane beyond the Curtain, killed a vengeful ghost, and met **[[Eleanor Gray]]** — [[Aldric]]'s mother, a ghost who named him after King Aldrich, called him "Aldrick of Havenport," and told him he was never a mistake. She had recently escaped the enthrallment of the spirit behind the black veil, a putrid entity that commands the undead. Azrith jumped back through the Curtain and rejoined the party. Back in the undercroft, [[Samantha Westermere]] revealed that Beowyn and Eodwin Wulfhelm were friends with her family — until Wulfhelm killed them all. The truth lies beyond the veil. The party studied the veil and learned that radiant energy repels it and that something vital may need to be exchanged to pass through.

**[[2026.10.06]] "The Price of Passage"** (session 16)
Odine's natural 20 revealed the black veil's terms: break it with a divine smite beyond any mortal's power, or "knock" and pay with life force, 10 hit points of maximum health shared among the party. The Oathkeeper [[Ian]] is forbidden by his order to set foot in the undercroft, and [[Mauve Marigold]] admitted that priests and a [[House Corwyn]] hedge mage had already failed against it. She offered her own blood but would not ask strangers, or the dying, to pay. Aldric still doubts the ghost is really his mother, and Cassian balks at the cost. After salty Harbor Gose at the [[Salt House]], Odine called in her favor, and the Crusaders resolved to face the veil in the morning.

**[[2026.10.13]] "Beyond the Black Veil"** (session 17)
All four players present. After breakfast at the Salt House (and a free knob of garlic from [[Edith Rooke]]), the party gave up on finding a substitute sacrifice: the price needed a conscious, willing subject. Azrith realised that if Odine paid alone she would be stranded beyond the veil, so all four touched the wall together and paid the toll — nine points of life force shared, and Odine's final point to break the seal. Beyond lay the ethereal domain of death: a corridor of trapped souls begging release from the grasp of **[[The Warden of Death]]**, opening into a burial chamber of nine deity statues with the **Shepherd** — death's archetype — enthroned. The souls animated the Shepherd statue; it greeted the party as "little flames" and attacked. The party destroyed the statue (it was undead, charm-immune, fire-resistant), but the Warden's ethereal rostrum survived — *"you have destroyed my body, but you will not break my oath"* — and drifted toward another statue to possess. The session ended on that cliffhanger.

**[[2026.11.18]] "The Warden Falls"** (session 18)
All four players present, after a month's break. The fight resumed mid-round: the party raced the Warden's rostrum for the eight remaining deity statues, and Odine, Cassian and Aldric shattered them all between catapult, eldritch blasts and greatsword. Enraged — *"fifteen years I've sheltered them... I was their shepherd"* — it tried a **desperate possession** of Odine, pouring its mist into her; her natural-20 Charisma save (heroic inspiration spent) turned the attack back, and 10 radiant damage from her ancient magic annihilated it (*"No! You were meant to be mine!"*). The trapped souls were freed, and the black veil and the Curtain vanished. In the burial chamber the party found the Westermere treasury — **5,200 gp**, a **necklace of prayer beads** (Odine), a **cloak of displacement** (Cassian) and a **ceremonial mace** (Aldric) — and Azrith's scimitars were blessed to +2 by part of **[[Eleanor Gray]]'s** essence. **[[Samantha Westermere]]** drifted in, confirmed **the Shepherd statue was a gift-trap from [[House Wulfhelm]], who deliberately spread the Wither**, and passed on. Above ground the storm broke, but the Wither-sick at the Salt House were not cured. The party gave 500 gp to Havenport's community and won **[[Mauve Marigold]]'s** pledge of Havenport's support for Blackwood's cause. Everyone reached **level 6**; *greater restoration* from Odine's necklace restored all the life force the veil had taken. **[[Captain Garran]]** escorted the Crusaders back to Everdale, ten days before the final contest, where **[[Duke Tristan Blackwood]]** revealed that someone *"has already decided"* Erovast's future and the contest is how they intend to make it happen — and proposed the party **compete from the shadows**.

## Where Things Stand

- **The Warden of Death is destroyed (2026.11.18).** Odine's ancient magic turned its desperate possession back on itself; the trapped souls of Havenport were freed, the black veil and the Curtain vanished, and **[[Samantha Westermere]] passed on**. See [[The Warden of Death]], [[Confront the Spirit Behind the Black Veil]] (resolved).
- **House Wulfhelm deliberately spread the Wither** and gifted the Shepherd statue to [[House Westermere]] as a trap — confirmed by Samantha. The Wither still grips the living: the sick at the [[Salt House]] were not cured. See [[Uncover the Westermere-Wulfhelm Coup]], [[Investigate the Wither in Havenport]].
- **The party is back in Everdale** (escorted home by [[Captain Garran]]), all level 6, life force restored via *greater restoration* from Odine's **necklace of prayer beads**. Ten days remain to the **final contest**; the fairgrounds are militarized and the party stands somewhere between disgraced and controversial.
- **[[Duke Tristan Blackwood]] believes someone has already decided Erovast's future**, and the final contest is how they intend to make it happen. He proposes the party **compete from the shadows**; his plan (plus backup and plan C) is to be laid out next session. See [[Compete in the King's Tourney]].
- **[[Mauve Marigold]] and Havenport will hear Blackwood out** and back the party — *"if you need Havenport's help, then you'll have mine."*
- House Godrin is chasing Odine's decoy toward the western Vale, where Jevon has gone to mislead them; see [[Investigate House Godrin]].
- The Wither spreads from the [[Thornwood Vale]]. Its link to the Dark King, the kneeling statue and Odine's lineage is the campaign's central mystery; see [[Investigate the Pestilence]].
- Cassian's dream bargain with [[The Masked Figure]] is still unresolved, as is the question of why [[House Wulfhelm]] will not acknowledge [[House Fortescue]].
- New treasures from the Westermere treasury: the **necklace of prayer beads** (Odine), the **cloak of displacement** (Cassian), the **ceremonial mace** (Aldric), and Azrith's **+2 scimitars** blessed by [[Eleanor Gray]].
- [[Aldric]]'s mother is the ghost [[Eleanor Gray]], trapped in the ethereal plane. She named him "Aldrick of Havenport" and always meant to bring him home; part of her essence now empowers Azrith's blades.
