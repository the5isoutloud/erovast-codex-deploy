# The Black Veil

![[Black_Veil.png]]

## Summary

The black veil is an impenetrable barrier in the undercroft of [[Westermere Manor]], sealing off an ethereal domain beyond. It appeared the moment [[House Wulfhelm]] killed the [[House Westermere]] family 15 years ago — all that remained visible was "a black wall of force" blocking the undercroft. Source: [[2026.09.22|2026.09.22]], [[2026.09.29|2026.09.29]].

## Notes

- The veil is impenetrable to [[Samantha Westermere]], who "learned more in the last five minutes than I have in the last five years" just watching others touch it.
- It is tied to the [[Curtain]], the gateway between the ethereal and material planes beneath the manor.
- [[Eleanor Gray]] was enthralled by the spirit on the other side of the veil — the same dark force it seals off.
- The veil's appearance coincided with the Westermere family's disappearance and the onset of the [[Wither]] in Havenport.
- [[House Wulfhelm]]'s coup against the Westermeres may be connected to the veil, though the exact relationship is unknown.

## Relationships
