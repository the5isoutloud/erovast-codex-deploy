# The Masked Figure

## Summary

An unnamed figure that appears to [[Cassian]] in his dreams. It wears a trimmed gown and regalia and a mask that conceals nearly its entire face, revealing only mischievous eyes. It speaks with intelligence, amusement, and a preference for long-term loyalties over coin. It gifted Cassian his magical dice in a prior dream—the same dice that trails a burgundy light when rolled. Source: [[00 - Game Log/2026.05.19|2026.05.19]].

## Current Status

Active. A deal was struck with Cassian during the long rest before the first Proving Grounds bout. The figure's identity and true nature are unknown. Source: [[00 - Game Log/2026.05.19|2026.05.19]].

## The Deal (struck 2026.05.19)

**Cassian's obligations:**
- Continue stirring trouble among the noble houses.
- Keep the party together through the tournament.
- Keep [[Odine Dunmere]] close: *"I find her interesting and relevant; don't let her out of your sight."*

**The Masked Figure's obligations:**
- Pull strings to remove *"immovable roadblocks"* in the tournament—named as [[Ser Calder Ross]] (House Wulfhelm's champion) and [[House Godrin]]'s champion.
- Find the answer to how Cassian's family acquired the dice and deliver it before the final bout.

Source: [[00 - Game Log/2026.05.19|2026.05.19]].

## Notes

- The figure called Cassian *"clever boy"* and said: *"I don't like to trade in gloria coin. I prefer loyalties—skills ones that cannot be bought."*
- It framed the arrangement as a *"partnership"* rather than an employer/employee relationship.
- It expressed interest in both Cassian's party and specifically in Odine; it found the presence of two tieflings together *"interesting."*
- It does not know how Cassian's family acquired the dice—this is a genuine mystery to it as well.
- Dream appearances take place in mundane locations (the [[Ragged Flagon]]); whether this is Cassian's subconscious or a true visitation is unknown.

## Relationships

- [[Cassian]]: The primary contact. It gifted him his dice and has appeared in at least two separate dreams.
- [[Odine Dunmere]]: The figure considers her *"interesting and relevant"* for unknown reasons.
