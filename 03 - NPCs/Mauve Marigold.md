# Mauve Marigold

## Summary

Mauve Marigold is the person [[Duke Tristan Blackwood]] sent the party to find in [[Havenport]]. They are to stay with her until he sends word. She has not yet been met. **Naming note:** the GM said "Mauve Mauve Marigold" in the recap, and [[Ian]] said "Mav," so the exact spelling is unconfirmed. Source: [[2026.09.22|2026.09.22]].

## Notes

- Per Ian, "Mav and her people" have taken refuge in the village from the undead beneath [[Westermere Manor]]. She may be among the survivors at the Salt House, but that is unconfirmed.

## Relationships

- [[Duke Tristan Blackwood]]: Directed the party to her.
- [[Havenport]]: Lives there with "her people."
