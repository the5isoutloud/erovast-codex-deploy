# Confront the Spirit Behind the Black Veil

## Status

Active. A subquest of [[Havenport]]. On [[2026.09.29|2026.09.29]], [[Azrith]] returned from the ethereal plane and met [[Eleanor Gray]] — [[Aldric]]'s mother, a ghost trapped beyond the black veil. She told him about the spirit behind the veil: *"a putrid and foul entity... an evil force, a spirit that commands the undead. It draws it unto itself."* Source: [[2026.09.29|2026.09.29]].

Azrith declared: *"if it's undead and he can kill it, he will."* Source: [[2026.09.29|2026.09.29]].

On [[2026.10.06|2026.10.06]], [[Odine Dunmere]]'s natural 20 revealed the veil's price: **10 HP of maximum hit points** of shared life force (narratively, about a year of life split among four). Neither [[Ian]] nor [[Mauve Marigold]] could offer a divine alternative. [[Aldric]] and Odine each offered 5 HP, and [[Cassian]] remains reluctant. The party rested at the [[Salt House]] and planned to return to the veil in the morning; Odine vowed to go *"with or without you."* Source: [[2026.10.06|2026.10.06]].

## The Spirit

- Commands the undead.
- Entices spirits — it enthralled Eleanor Gray before she escaped its control.
- Is why the Curtains are acting strangely and why spirits dying in Havenport are trapped.
- Eleanor refuses to speak its name: *"I dare not speak its name for it will invoke its wrath."* She has avoided its ire for a few years since escaping. Source: [[2026.09.29|2026.09.29]].
- It is beyond the black veil in the Westermere Manor undercroft.

## What's Known About the Veil

- It is a shimmering black wall sealing off part of the undercroft, ever since the Curtain opened. Source: [[2026.09.22|2026.09.22]].
- It is impenetrable to [[Samantha Westermere]] (a ghost). Source: [[2026.09.22|2026.09.22]].
- To the living: it feels like cold steel, and black mist crawls up the arm stealing warmth. It doesn't feel evil — it feels like **entropy**. Source: [[2026.09.22|2026.09.22]].
- It is not elven magic. It feels foreign and alien — the ethereal and material planes, normally separate, are converging here. Source: [[2026.09.22|2026.09.22]].
- It reacts to living creatures — there's a sense of refusal. Whatever is on the other side is protecting that space from outsiders. Source: [[2026.09.29|2026.09.29]].
- Radiant energy staves off its negative effects. Source: [[2026.09.29|2026.09.29]].
- Passing through may require enough radiant energy, or exchanging something vital with the necrotic force. Source: [[2026.09.29|2026.09.29]].
- It is old, complex, and not understood magic. Source: [[2026.09.29|2026.09.29]].
- **Two ways through** (Odine's natural 20 Insight): (1) a divine smite large enough to beat what seems to be an alien, near-infinite necrotic power source, which is probably beyond the party; or (2) "knocking": the veil invites them in for a price of **10 HP of maximum HP** in life force, which can be shared. Source: [[2026.10.06|2026.10.06]].
- Past attempts failed: priests prayed at it and tried to bless the burial site, and a [[House Corwyn]] hedge mage spent three days on it without success. Source: [[2026.10.06|2026.10.06]].
- The [[Oathkeepers]] know of it and have forbidden [[Ian]] to go down there because of missing persons. Source: [[2026.10.06|2026.10.06]].
- The wall swallows light completely (shown by [[Aldric]]'s Light cantrip). Source: [[2026.09.22|2026.09.22]].

## What Azrith Learned in the Ethereal Plane

On [[2026.09.29|2026.09.29]], Azrith fought through the ethereal plane:

- Killed a **vengeful ghost** (wraith-type: horrific visage, withering touch) — it had been killed by something and was angry about it. Source: [[2026.09.29|2026.09.29]].
- Met **[[Eleanor Gray]]** hiding behind a tomb. She led him to a ledge over an ethereal abyss and showed him how to return. Source: [[2026.09.29|2026.09.29]].
- Azrith jumped from the ledge and crashed back into the Westermere Manor undercroft. Source: [[2026.09.29|2026.09.29]].
