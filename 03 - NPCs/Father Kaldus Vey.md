# Father Kaldus Vey

## Summary

Father Kaldus Vey is a middle-aged priest of the Royal Chapel working the healer tents at the King's Fair. He treated **[[Aldrich]]**'s exhaustion after the King's Cup, casting Greater Restoration (capped at three castings per day) for a fee of 100 gp in diamond dust plus a 300 gp materials cost, sweetened by a 100 gp donation from [[Cassian]]—400 gp total. Source: [[00 - Game Log/2026.08.25|2026.08.25]].

## Notes

- Warm and welcoming, quick to invite passersby in "to pray."
- Cares for patients overnight in his sanctuary; made Aldrich a makeshift bed and dressed him in spare Royal Chapel robes after his ruined clothes were discarded.
- Curious about Aldrich's tiefling heritage, gently probing about his "blood curse" (red skin, horns) and offering a "path of redemption" without pressing when Aldrich brushed it off.
- Mentioned that House Oswall clerics have recently moved into the fairgrounds as an alternative option, and that tournament healers exist but are normally gated behind noble access or steep fees.

## Relationships

- [[Aldrich]]: Treated his exhaustion over the course of a night and morning.
- [[Cassian]]: Negotiated payment with him directly.
