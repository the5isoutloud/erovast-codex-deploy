# Gleaming Keep

## Summary

The Gleaming Keep crowns the hill above [[Everdale]]. According to local lore, King Aldrich and his powerful mages transmuted the keep's stonework into metal so it would endure, causing it to gleam in the light. Source: [[00 - Game Log/2026.05.05|2026.05.05]].

## Related Lore

- The keep houses or protects the [[Empty Throne]], which has remained unfilled since King Aldrich.
- The [[Oathkeepers]] guard the keep and preserve it for the hoped-for return of King Aldrich.
- The [[King's Tourney]] takes place in the valley below the keep.
