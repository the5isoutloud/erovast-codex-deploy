# The Menagerie

## Summary

The Menagerie is the tournament tier above the [[King's Tourney|Proving Grounds]]: a gladiatorial "fantasy bullfighting" arena where captured monsters are released into the ring for champions to fight, in front of thousands of spectators—far larger crowds than the Proving Grounds draws. Source: [[2026.08.11|2026.08.11]].

## Notes

- Posters advertise past creatures fought here: a steel-plated gorgon-bull, a medusa-type monster, and a griffon.
- Judged on **spectacle, daring, and mastery**, not just on killing the beast—per [[Holt]]: "any butcher can kill an animal."
- A bloodied, stretcher-carried adventurer was seen leaving the tent area, underscoring the increased danger over the Proving Grounds.
- **Cassian's Crushing Crusaders** advanced here after completing the [[Crown Gauntlet]]. Their bout is scheduled the day after [[2026.08.11|2026.08.11]] at 5 PM, assigned the red tent, bracketed near a [[House Wulfhelm]]-affiliated opponent (red vs. blue tent). [[House Oswall]] also registered several unarmored entrants this year.
- Bouts here pit champions against released monsters rather than other champions directly. On [[2026.08.25|2026.08.25]], Cassian's Unseen Servant scouted the previous bracket match—four black knights fighting a beast with a deep, lion-like roar, sketched by the servant as a **manticore**. The party then walked out to face a fresh manticore of their own; Odine's opening Intimidation check failed to faze it, and combat/initiative was deferred to the next session. Source: [[2026.08.25|2026.08.25]].
- **The manticore bout ended in an unprecedented way** on [[2026.09.08|2026.09.08]]: rather than killing the beast, [[Cassian]] talked it down mid-combat, discovered it was a captive controlled by a trebuchet-and-net failsafe, and freed it—flying it to disable the trebuchet and letting it fly off unharmed. The arena's crowd-approval mechanic (a per-round d20 group check against a hidden DC) turned against the party as the fight dragged on without a kill; officials could not agree on how to score the bout, and the paying crowd largely booed, though peasants watching from a nearby cliffside outside the arena cheered the party as heroes. See [[The Freed Manticore]].
