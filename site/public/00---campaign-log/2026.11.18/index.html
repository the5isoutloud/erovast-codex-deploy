2026.11.18#

Summary#

All four players were present this session (after a long out-of-character opening — it had been over a month since 2026.10.13). The Weaver of Fate recapped the cliffhanger: the party had passed the Black Veil, destroyed the Warden’s Shepherd statue, and its ethereal rostrum was drifting toward another of the nine deity statues. The fight resumed mid-round on Cassian’s turn. Source: Transcript.

The statue race. Cassian sprinted to Odine’s side. Odine quickened a catapult (2 sorcery points) to fling a statue fragment for 16 bludgeoning, then spent her action on eldritch blast — four beams (she later noted she had mistakenly rolled four, overkill for statues that break at a threshold of 10) shattering the middle and right-hand statues. She backed away, pulling Cassian with her. Azrith closed and hit the rostrum with a scimitar (8 radiant), then took an opportunity attack as it drifted (12 radiant). The Warden’s 60-foot wave of divine dread forced Wisdom saves — Aldric (17), Azrith (16) and Cassian (17) all saved; Odine was frightened (though her retroactive save, a natural 20, freed her on her next turn). It hissed “your souls will be mine” and moved toward the remaining statues. Source: Transcript.

Aldric charged in and hacked at it with his greatsword (9 damage through its physical resistance), then action surged for a second slashing combo (7 more). Cassian’s twin eldritch blasts (10 and 14) shattered two more statues; Odine’s blasts — a natural 20 and a 17 — pulverised the last one, and she converted a level-2 slot back into sorcery points. With every vessel gone the Warden raged: “You would unmake what I have kept. Fifteen years I’ve sheltered them from oblivion. Not one soul forgotten. Not one surrendered. I was their shepherd. Without me, who will keep them?” Source: Transcript.

Desperate possession. Azrith’s chill touch harmlessly washed over it — the Warden is immune to necrotic damage — and it cursed him (“you seek to take life from one who is already dead”). It drifted toward Odine (Aldric’s opportunity attack: a natural 20 for 8 through resistance) and attempted a desperate possession, pouring its mist into her to take her body from within. Odine spent a heroic inspiration on the DC 15 Charisma save and rolled a natural 20: her ancient magic violently rejected the Warden, dealing 10 radiant damage and destroying it. Her black vine-marks flashed radiant as she screamed the mist out of the room; the Warden dissipated with a last cry: “No! You were meant to be mine!” Source: Transcript, The Warden of Death.

The curse is lifted. The mist — thousands of souls trapped over the years — dissipated, and looking back the party saw the black veil was gone. Odine collapsed; Cassian ran over and cast Healing Hands (10 HP) on her for the first time. Leaving, they found the ethereal Curtain gone too: just a quiet stone undercroft. The Warden would no longer draw the souls of Havenport’s dead. Source: Transcript, Black Veil.

Loot. A party investigation (Azrith’s 22 carried the roll) uncovered ornate chests in the ruined treasury: 5,200 gold pieces (which the party kept, minus gifts below) and three Westermere artifacts:

  • a necklace of prayer beads (six gemstone beads, each holding a once-per-dawn bonus-action spell; Odine claimed it). Rolled beads: bless, two slots of level-2 cure wounds, shining smite, greater restoration (re-rolled on Azrith’s heroic inspiration), and a bead of wind walking (“Blessing of the Mariner”). The GM overruled the item’s class/attunement restrictions for the campaign.
  • a cloak of displacement (fey trickster make, golden trim, strange stone-spiked tassels) — Cassian took and mastered it during downtime.
  • a ceremonial mace, a rare artifact that glows and deals an extra 2d6 radiant to fiends and undead, destroys such a target at 25 HP or fewer on a failed DC 15 Wisdom save (frightened even on a success) — Aldric claimed it, chasing Azrith around the chamber with it.

Eleanor’s blessing. Azrith’s scimitars began glowing with ethereal light: a presence animated them, and he felt a sense of gratitude — part of Eleanor Gray’s essence empowers them. They are now +2 scimitars (the silvered one remains silvered). Source: Transcript, Eleanor Gray.

Samantha passes on. Samantha Westermere drifted in, knelt at the skeletal remains of her family (concealed until now beneath the mist), and named the truth: House Wulfhelm gifted the Shepherd statue to her family — it was intended as a trap. Wulfhelm deliberately spread the Wither and twisted the divine being (a veil warden meant to ferry souls onward) into a soul-magnet that drew Havenport’s dead here. “You must beware the ambitions of the noble houses.” She sensed the ancient magic in Odine, said she would find her own way now that the monster was gone, spoke the parting blessing “May the Mariner watch over you all”, and dissipated — at rest. Source: Transcript, Uncover the Westermere-Wulfhelm Coup.

Havenport after. Emerging, the party found the storm had finally broken — light cracking over Havenport, the air less oppressive. But at the Salt House, Edith Rooke was still tending the Wither-sick: the battle was won, but the curse on the living was not undone. The party told the tale; Edith owed them a round. They gave 500 gold to the community (200 from Cassian, 300 from Odine), and Mauve Marigold — who had lived with the ghosts for 15 years — declared “something’s changed up there”: she doesn’t trust “that slimy bastard” Blackwood but is willing to hear him, and if the party needs Havenport’s help, they have hers. Source: Transcript.

Downtime and level 6. Over two days of rest, Odine attuned to the necklace and cast greater restoration on Cassian, then Aldric, then Azrith (100 gp of components each, from party funds) — all the life force sapped by the veil was restored. The party reached level 6 (granted after Azrith’s persuasion check of 5 on the DM): Odine took Warcaster; Cassian gained two Misty Step tricks from his archfey patron — Disappearing (invisible on stepping) and Dreadful (2d10 psychic damage). Source: Transcript.

Return to Everdale. Captain Garran returned with a sealed letter from Duke Tristan Blackwood and escorted the party back through the teleportation circle to the Gleaming Keep. Ten days remain to the final contest. The fairgrounds had changed: soldiers where entertainers stood, armed patrols, new noble banners, hushed crowds — “more crowd, but less noise” — a kingdom holding its breath. The party’s standing has slipped from famous to somewhere between disgraced and controversial; a wrong step could make them public enemies. Renny Blackwood ushered them in (unnerved by ghost talk). The Duke hugged Cassian and Odine, patted Azrith’s head, revealed Mauve is willing to “play ball”, and confessed the tourney’s true stakes: “I thought we were competing to determine the future of Erovast. I was wrong. Someone has already decided its future — and the contest is how they intend to make it happen.” He has a plan, a backup, and a plan C, and proposes the party compete from the shadows. The session ended with the party sent to rest; plans in the morning. Source: Transcript, Duke Tristan Blackwood, Compete in the King's Tourney.

Open Threads#

  • The final contest (10 days): someone has already decided Erovast’s future; the contest is how they intend to make it happen. Blackwood’s plan (and plan B and C) will be laid out next session.
  • Compete from the shadows: the party is out of the Menagerie and cannot compete under normal pretenses; Blackwood proposes covert participation.
  • The Wither still grips the living: the Warden’s death freed Havenport’s dead, but the sick at the Salt House are still afflicted, and the Wither itself still spreads.
  • Wulfhelm’s guilt is confirmed: the Wither was spread deliberately, and the Shepherd statue was a gift-trap. What does the party do with this truth, and who will believe them?
  • Party standing: disgraced-to-controversial at the tourney; one misstep from becoming public enemies.
  • The Mariner: Samantha’s parting blessing names a deity (“May the Mariner watch over you”) — not previously spoken of; possibly one of the nine.
  • The necklace’s beads and the mace are powerful but partly unexamined (the mace’s full stat block was pasted to Aldric out of character).
