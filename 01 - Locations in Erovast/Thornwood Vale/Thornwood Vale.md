# Thornwood Vale

## Summary

The Thornwood Vale, usually just called **"the Vale,"** is a forested valley behind the [[Gleaming Keep]]. It is the home of [[Odine Dunmere]] and her people, and the source of the blight known as **the Wither** (also called the pestilence or the hollowing). It is also the grand prize of the [[King's Tourney]]. The corruption there affects plants, animals and people, and it has now spread into the Crownlands. Source: [[2026.05.05|2026.05.05]], [[2026.08.18|2026.08.18]].

> [!note] Naming note
> Speech-to-text usually writes "Vale" as **"veil"**. A transcript audit (2026-09-25) confirmed that every place-reference to "the veil" in play means this Vale. That includes the home of [[Gruvelda Duskbelt]]'s dwarven clan. The only literal "veil" so far is the black wall in the [[Westermere Manor]] undercroft.

## Geography

The Vale is a long, narrow forested canyon with black cliff sides and mountains rising to the east. From Everdale's hilltop it looks veiled in an almost imperceptible green mist. It was once the king's hunting grounds. Source: [[2026.05.05|2026.05.05]], [[2026.05.12|2026.05.12]].

## Peoples

- **The Guardians of the Vale:** Odine's elven people, an isolated, matriarchal community. They stop aging while they remain in the Vale. There are *"countless villages inside the Vale."* See [[Odine Dunmere]]. Source: [[2026.08.25|2026.08.25]].
- **The Duskbelt dwarves:** [[Gruvelda Duskbelt]]'s clan lives in the Vale. Her brother [[Grimhall Duskbelt]] protects them from shadows and ghouls that *"make their way down into the Vale,"* a problem he blames on the town above rather than the Vale itself. Source: [[2026.05.12|2026.05.12]], [[2026.05.19|2026.05.19]].
- **A dwarven clan that works with [[House Godrin]]** supplies Godrin's mana crystals. It is not confirmed whether this is the Duskbelt clan. Source: [[2026.06.23|2026.06.23]].
- **[[Maralynn]]** is from the Vale and is said to have *"returned to her people in the Vale."* House Godrin has confirmed she is alive. Source: [[2026.05.12|2026.05.12]], [[2026.06.23|2026.06.23]], [[2026.08.25|2026.08.25]].

## Political Importance

- **The tourney's grand prize** is the King's Cup together with the deed and title to the Vale. Source: [[2026.05.05|2026.05.05]], [[2026.06.11|2026.06.11]], [[2026.06.23|2026.06.23]].
- [[House Wulfhelm]] won last year and holds the deed. It and House Godrin have traded the deed back and forth for about a decade. Source: [[2026.05.05|2026.05.05]], [[2026.08.18|2026.08.18]].
- [[House Corwyn]] believes the Vale hides a secret that could unite the lesser houses against Godrin and Wulfhelm. Source: [[2026.08.18|2026.08.18]].
- **Mana crystals and spell draught:** whoever holds the deed can mine the Vale's **mana crystals**. House Godrin uses them to power its arcane arsenal. They can also be transmuted into **spell draught**, the liquid that powers the Oathkeepers' surviving teleportation circles. Source: [[2026.06.23|2026.06.23]], [[2026.09.22|2026.09.22]].
- Odine insists *"no one can hold the title to the Vale."* Source: [[2026.08.11|2026.08.11]].

## The Blight and the Entity

- The pestilence is tied to the mist entity. [[Odine Dunmere]]'s grandmother Mara, one of the Touched, sacrificed herself to cast the entity back. It is now as strong as it was then, and the blight is spreading faster than ever. Source: [[2026.06.11|2026.06.11]].
- The humans' story of the King defeating the hollowing about 150 years ago is, in Odine's word, *"Lies."* Source: [[2026.06.11|2026.06.11]].
- **The kneeling statue:** in the Oathkeepers' legend, all that was left of King Aldrich after he defeated the **Dark King** was *"a statue of himself"* in the Vale. Odine has seen it: a human king kneeling with his sword in the ground, alone in the forest. See [[Investigate the Pestilence]]. Source: [[2026.09.22|2026.09.22]].

## Known Details

- [[Minnie]]'s huckleberry mead is made from huckleberries harvested on the fringes of the Vale. Odine was surprised that people from Everdale risk going that close. Source: [[2026.05.05|2026.05.05]].
- The corrupted horse the party met before reaching Everdale suggested the curse is no longer confined to the Vale. Source: [[2026.05.05|2026.05.05]].
- [[Jevon]] has gone to an outpost on the Vale's western fringe to help mislead the House Godrin scouts chasing Odine's "Erendel Morath" decoy. The decoy's fake location is also deep in the Vale. Source: [[2026.08.25|2026.08.25]], [[2026.09.15|2026.09.15]].
