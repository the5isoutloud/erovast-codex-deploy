# Oathkeepers

## Summary

The Oathkeepers are three orders of knights bound under one oath in [[Everdale]]. They guard the [[Gleaming Keep]], protect the [[Empty Throne]], police the [[King's Tourney]], and help oversee the city's economy and settlement. Source: [[2026.05.05|2026.05.05]].

## The Three Orders

The Oathkeepers are composed of three distinct knightly orders, each with its own banner, that agreed to a shared code and merged their purpose after King Aldrich's reign ended. They are the only three orders that still survive from that era. Source: [[2026.05.12|2026.05.12]].

| Banner | Emblem | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Green shield | A stag / antlers | The same order as Sir Televast, the knight the party encountered before reaching Everdale |
| Red shield | An ember flame | — |
| Silver crest | A helm with two crossed swords | — |

The orders patrol [[Everdale]]'s hamlet streets in pairs and wear their own emblems. [[Cassian]] recognized all three as the oldest surviving knightly orders in the current era of men. Source: [[2026.05.12|2026.05.12]].

## Belief

The Oathkeepers preserve the keep and throne because they believe King Aldrich will someday return to rule again. They tell children this as a bedtime story—that the king will emerge from the stone and unite all the realms. Source: [[2026.05.05|2026.05.05]], [[2026.05.12|2026.05.12]].

## Internal Dissent

In an untranscribed session before [[2026.08.11|2026.08.11]], the party learned that one of the Oathkeeper knights they met on the road into Everdale is privately fed up with the order's oath-bound inability to act against a new blight called **the Wither**—the same phenomenon the party has been calling "the hollowing." This is the first time the blight has been given a proper name in play. See [[Investigate the Pestilence]]. Source: [[2026.08.11|2026.08.11]].
