Westermere Manor#

Summary#

Westermere Manor is the cliffside keep of the fallen House Westermere, overlooking Havenport. Like the Gleaming Keep, its walls were transmuted into metal, but here the metal is tarnished and neglected; the manor looks 10–15 years unkept. It holds Havenport’s teleportation circle, watched by the lone Oathkeeper Ian. Beneath it is an undercroft where the ethereal and material planes are converging. Source: 2026.09.22.

The Teleportation Circle#

The circle is one of a few that survive from King Aldrich’s time, linked to the Gleaming Keep’s. To use it:

  1. Pour spell draught into the lines of the circle. Spell draught is a bluish-silver liquid transmuted from the Thornwood Vale mana crystals that House Godrin harvests.
  2. Hold the plate-sized metal keystone in the center of the circle.
  3. Speak the destination’s name.

Use of the circle is paid for with tribute to the Oathkeepers. Duke Tristan Blackwood paid roughly 5,000 gp for the party’s trip. Ian took the keystone back when they arrived. Source: 2026.09.22.

The Undercroft#

Stairwells on either side of the entrance hall lead down to the undercroft. Samantha Westermere, the family’s ghost, says “much of it has fallen into the ethereal realm.” Ian says undead are infesting it, and he keeps away with the help of an amulet. Village children sometimes wander up at night. Source: 2026.09.22.

  • The Curtain: a floating sphere of swirling black mist blocking the archway from the first large room into a hallway. It whispered “door” to Odine Dunmere. Samantha calls it “a curtain to the realm of death,” a passage between the ethereal and material planes.
    • It opens and closes unpredictably and tends to close after it is used.
    • Both the living and the dead use it, “some of them are vile.” Traffic has grown as more people have died in recent years.
    • Azrith leapt into it and vanished. See Find Azrith.
  • The black veil: a shimmering black wall that has sealed off part of the undercroft since the Curtain opened. It wasn’t there in Samantha’s lifetime, and it is impenetrable to her.
    • To a mortal it feels like cold steel. Black mist crawls up the arm and steals warmth, like entropy, “almost like it’s turning your essence into living shadow.” It is not evil in itself.
    • It swallows light with no reflection. Radiant light (Odine’s Sacred Flame) drives the shadow back.
    • Cassian’s magic check (natural 20) found that no one is casting a spell there. The wall itself is the source, and “whatever lies beyond that wall is affecting you.”
  • Samantha’s room: where she died.

Relationships#

  • House Westermere: The family that owned it.
  • Samantha Westermere: Haunts the undercroft.
  • Ian: The Oathkeeper posted at the circle.
  • Havenport: The town below.
  • Gleaming Keep: Linked by the teleportation circle.
