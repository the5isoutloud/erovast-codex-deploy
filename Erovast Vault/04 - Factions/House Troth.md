# House Troth

## Summary

House Troth is a minor, lesser-known vassal house of [[House Wulfhelm]]. **[[Aldrich]]** once served under them—not as nobility, but in a rank-and-file guard/security capacity—before leaving "the seaside" to see the world at an old friend's encouragement. Source: [[2026.08.18|2026.08.18]].

## Notes

- Confirmed live at the table as Aldrich's former house (chosen between two options offered—House Troth and House Hawk). This is the same house previously tracked in the wiki only as an unnamed [[House Wulfhelm]] vassal—this session finally gave it a name.
- [[Duke Tristan Blackwood]] advised Aldrich to keep the connection quiet; Aldrich is confident no one at the tournament would recognize him from it.

## Relationships

- [[House Wulfhelm]]: Liege house.
- [[Aldrich]]: Former low-ranking guard.
