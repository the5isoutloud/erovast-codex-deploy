# Beowyn Wulfhelm

## Summary

Beowyn Wulfhelm was a lord or prince of [[House Wulfhelm]] and a close friend of the lord of [[House Westermere]]. He traded with the Westermeres, supplied them with useful exports, and allied with them during the great war. He was proud to know the Westermere lord, and the Westermere lord was proud to know him. Beowyn's current wife had a child who would later visit the Westermeres in the summers — Beowyn knew the Westermere family well. Source: [[2026.09.29|2026.09.29]].

## Notes

- Beowyn and his brother Eodwin Wulfhelm learned something **dire about House Wulfhelm** — a secret they hoped to keep quiet.
- The Westermere family discovered this secret.
- House Wulfhelm realized the Westermeres knew, and staged a trap.
- The Westermere family was killed in an instant; all that remained was a black wall of force blocking the undercroft of [[Westermere Manor]].
- This was 15 years before [[2026.09.29|2026.09.29]]. The Wither began creeping into Havenport afterward.
- The truth about what Beowyn and Eodwin discovered lies beyond the black veil.
