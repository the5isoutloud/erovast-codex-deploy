# The Spirit Behind the Veil

## Summary

A putrid and foul spirit that commands the undead, residing beyond the black veil in the [[Westermere Manor]] undercroft. It is an evil force from the ethereal plane that enthralls spirits and draws the dead to itself. [[Eleanor Gray]] escaped its control after being enthralled for an unknown period. Its name is too dangerous to speak. Source: [[2026.09.29|2026.09.29]].

## Notes

- Described by Eleanor Gray as "a putrid and foul entity beyond that veil, an evil force, a spirit that commands the undead. It draws it unto itself."
- It resides in the ethereal plane but reaches into the mortal realm.
- It is the reason the Curtains are "acting strangely" — portals between the ethereal and material planes are unstable.
- It is why spirits dying in Havenport are trapped in this place rather than passing on.
- Eleanor was enthralled by it for an unknown period before recently escaping its control.
- She has taken "great care to avoid its ire and attention" since escaping.
- She refuses to speak its name: *"I dare not speak its name for it will invoke its wrath."*
- It is connected to the black veil: the veil seals off the undercroft and protects the space beyond from outsiders. Whatever is on the other side is the source of the veil.
- Eleanor asked [[Azrith]] if he would dare confront this spirit guardian and end its "putrid curse upon this land." Azrith agreed.
- The spirit's relationship to [[House Wulfhelm]]'s coup against [[House Westermere]] is unclear — Samantha said Wulfhelm killed her family, and Eleanor says the entity is behind the veil. Whether Wulfhelm unleashed it, encountered it, or was destroyed by it is unknown.
