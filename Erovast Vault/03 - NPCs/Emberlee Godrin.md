# Emberlee Godrin

## Summary

Emberlee Godrin is a member of [[House Godrin]]'s ruling family, named in [[Duke Tristan Blackwood]]'s intelligence note on the Godrin camp at [[White Feather Tor]]. She keeps mostly to the encampment and is rarely alone—making her a difficult infiltration target compared to other family members. Source: [[00 - Game Log/2026.08.18|2026.08.18]].

## Relationships

- [[House Godrin]]: Family member.
- [[Golstang Godrin]]: Presumed relative (patron).
- [[White Feather Tor]]: Rarely leaves the camp.
