# Kardis Wulfhelm

## Summary

Kardis Wulfhelm is a young noble, likely a prince of [[House Wulfhelm]], seen at [[Slug]]'s cookhouse during the [[King's Tourney]]. He has somewhat pointed ears, very long bright white hair, and wore shiny black armor. Source: [[00 - Game Log/2026.05.05|2026.05.05]].

## Notes

- [[Aldrich]] recognized Kardis by reputation and knew him to be reckless.
- [[Cassian]] knew House Wulfhelm won last year's tournament and currently controls the [[Thornwood Vale]].
- Kardis used an attendant to quietly move nearby patrons so he and his companion could have more space.
- He watched the party, especially Aldrich, and whispered to his black-armored companion.
- **Confirmed as the party's Menagerie-tier bracket rival**, per [[Holt]]'s bracket board and [[Rory]]'s reaction (2026.08.18). Also called "Cardus" in dialogue (same pronunciation/character). Prince and heir apparent of House Wulfhelm; per Rory, "one of the best" swordsmen in the house, though not the very best. Cassian privately calls him a "pompous prick." Both camps have reportedly been scouting each other unusually closely ahead of the bout. Source: [[00 - Game Log/2026.08.18|2026.08.18]].
- **Clarified (2026.08.25)**: the Menagerie pits contestants against released monsters, not rival champions directly, so the party does not fight Kardis in the ring—his team was bracketed near them and (per an Unseen Servant's scouting) fought a manticore in the match just before the party's own manticore bout. Source: [[00 - Game Log/2026.08.25|2026.08.25]].

## Relationships

- [[House Wulfhelm]]: Kardis is tied to the ruling family of the house.
- [[Aldrich]]: Aldrich once served a vassal house beneath House Wulfhelm.
- [[Compete in the King's Tourney]]: The party's confirmed Menagerie-bout opponent.
