# Marea Voss

## Summary

Marea Voss is an apothecary at the [[King's Tourney]]. She is a dark-skinned, sharp-tongued, witty woman with a silk gown, jewelry, and a mystic herbalist's style. Source: [[00 - Game Log/2026.05.05|2026.05.05]].

## Notes

- She sold four healing potions to [[Cassian]] and [[Odine Dunmere]] for 38 gold total after Cassian haggled her down.
- She sold [[Aldrich]] a Traveler's Tonic for 8 gold, granting advantage on a future ability check, saving throw, or attack.
- When Odine mentioned the pestilence in the [[Thornwood Vale]], Marea warned her to seek only true sources and avoid people who exploit misinformation. See [[Investigate the Pestilence]].
- On [[00 - Game Log/2026.08.11|2026.08.11]], sold [[Odine Dunmere]] two Potions of Greater Healing (150 gp each, haggled to 280 gp total) and negotiated with [[Cassian]] (Persuasion 23) to sell a rare **Potion of Hill Giant Strength** for 280 gp (down from 350 gp). She also keeps rarer stock on hand—a Potion of Growth, and the ingredients for a Potion of Speed (2,000–3,000 gp, declined) and a theoretical Potion of Flight requiring griffon plume or air-elemental essence.
