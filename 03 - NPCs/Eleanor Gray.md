# Eleanor Gray

## Summary

Eleanor Gray is the ghost of Aldric's mother, trapped in the ethereal plane beyond the Curtain in the Westermere Manor undercroft. She named her son after King Aldrich the Wise. She was enthralled by a dark force — the same spirit that commands the undead beyond the black veil — and recently escaped its control. She cannot return to the material realm but can communicate with those who pass through the Curtain. Source: [[2026.09.29|2026.09.29]].

## Notes

- She appeared to [[Azrith]] after he jumped through the Curtain and found himself in the ethereal plane.
- She hid behind a tomb when first encountered, whimpering and begging not to be harmed.
- Her son was born a tiefling — red skin, cute little horns — just like Aldric.
- She named him **Aldric** after King Aldrich, "the man who stood against the darkness."
- She called him **Aldrick of Havenport**, saying Havenport was "the first place I ever found where it didn't matter where you came from, you could find a sense of belonging."
- She wanted Aldric to know he was **never an accident** — there is no such thing as chance.
- She always meant to bring him home.
- She was enthralled by a dark force that resides in the mortal realm — the same entity behind the black veil.
- She recently escaped its enthrallment.
- She refers to the entity behind the veil as too dangerous to name: *"I dare not speak its name for it will invoke its wrath."*
- She describes the entity as "a putrid and foul entity beyond that veil, an evil force, a spirit that commands the undead. It draws it unto itself."
- She says this entity is why the Curtains are acting strangely and why spirits dying in Havenport are trapped.
- She showed [[Azrith]] how to return to the material realm: jump from a ledge into an ethereal abyss — the same way she last came through the Curtain.
- She was emotional when Azrith promised to tell Aldric her message, climbing up him and looking into his eyes.
- She asked if Aldric was safe, well, and happy. Azrith said he seemed fairly happy on the outside.
- She smiled with relief when she learned Aldric was not alone — he travels with friends.

## Relationships

- [[Aldric]]: Her son. She named him after King Aldrich.
- [[Azrith]]: The ghost-slayer who found her in the ethereal plane.
- [[Westermere Manor]]: The Curtain beneath it is the gate she last used to enter this plane.
- [[The Spirit Behind the Veil]]: The dark force that enthralled her.
- King Aldrich: She named her son after him.
