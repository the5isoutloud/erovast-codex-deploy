# Find Azrith

## Status

Active. In the undercroft of [[Westermere Manor]], [[Azrith]] took a running jump into **the Curtain**, a floating sphere of black mist that is a passage to "the realm of death." It shrank and vanished with him inside. Just before the party teleported to [[Havenport]], he had started to ask *"shouldn't we—"* and was cut off; the question is unanswered. Source: [[2026.09.22|2026.09.22]].

**Update 2026.09.29:** Azrith returned through the Curtain on [[2026.09.29|2026.09.29]]. In the ethereal plane he killed a vengeful ghost and met **[[Eleanor Gray]]**, who revealed she is [[Aldric]]'s mother. She named him after King Aldrich, called him "Aldrick of Havenport," and told him he was never a mistake. She was enthralled by the spirit behind the black veil and recently escaped. She won't speak its name. The quest remains active: the Curtain is still a dangerous gate, the spirit behind the veil commands the undead, and the truth about House Westermere's fall lies beyond the veil.

## Known Details

- Per [[Samantha Westermere]], Azrith may come back *"if he survives... when the curtain reopens."* That could be a day or a week, and the timing is unpredictable. The Curtain tends to close when it is used.
- Both the living and the dead pass through the Curtain, "some of them are vile." Traffic has grown as more people have died in recent years.
- Samantha has seen a group go through together.
- Azrith's player was absent this session, and the GM ran him.

## Next Steps

- Return to the undercroft once the Curtain reopens and decide whether to go through together.
- Learn more about the Curtain and the black veil beside it. Odine believes she can figure out the veil.
