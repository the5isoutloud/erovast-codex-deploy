Boarhead Tor#

Summary#

Boarhead Tor is the elevated noble camp hill at the King's Tourney grounds in Everdale. The great houses erect their finest silk tents here, stable prize horses, and post guards in house colors. It is cleaner and better-smelling than the common campgrounds, with music and revelry continuing even late at night. Source: 2026.05.19.

Notes#

  • House Wulfhelm maintains a significant presence here, marked by white and gold armor on their guards and banners posted throughout their section.
  • Kardis Wulfhelm reserved a full bedchamber tent for his return party the night of the party’s first visit.
  • Cassian fast-talked a noble servant into lending his personal tent for one night by playing up his vassal house connection to House Wulfhelm. The servant made clear it was a one-time offer and that the party was not welcome back after the tournament.
  • Access normally requires established house credentials; the servant warned that without them, the only alternatives are the common campgrounds or “Beggar’s Rook” (a step further down that even Cassian dismissed as an insult).

Relationships#

  • King's Tourney: Located within the tournament campgrounds.
  • House Wulfhelm: Primary noble house with visible presence on the hill.
  • Kardis Wulfhelm: Has reserved quarters here.
