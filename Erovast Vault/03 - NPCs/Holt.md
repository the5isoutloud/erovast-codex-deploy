# Holt

## Summary

Holt is a registrar at the [[King's Tourney]] registration tent. He wears a light blue and gold-trimmed cap and has a well-kept burly mustache. Source: [[2026.05.05|2026.05.05]].

## Notes

- He registered [[Cassian]], [[Aldrich]], [[Azrith]], and [[Odine Dunmere]] as **Cassian's Crushing Crusaders**.
- He charged the 25 gold group entry fee for the Proving Grounds.
- He placed the party's registration on the "to be watched" shelf.
- He scheduled the team to report to Tent D on the following in-game day at 6 PM. See [[Compete in the King's Tourney]].
- On [[2026.08.11|2026.08.11]], greeted the party warmly as "the talk of the fairgrounds," confirmed their promotion from the Proving Grounds to **The Menagerie** tier, and explained its judging emphasizes spectacle, daring, and mastery rather than simple kills ("any butcher can kill an animal"). Scheduled their bout for the next day at 5 PM in the red tent, opponent bracketed near [[House Wulfhelm]]. Also processed [[House Oswall]]'s unusually large tournament registration this year.
- On [[2026.08.18|2026.08.18]], confirmed via the public bracket board that the party's Menagerie opponent is **[[Kardis Wulfhelm]]** himself, sponsored under House Wulfhelm. Mentioned unusually heavy mutual scouting/interest between the two camps lately, but stayed coy about further gossip ("I only told them the best things"). Source: [[2026.08.18|2026.08.18]].
- Was **aghast** at the party's manticore bout, calling it "the most disrespectful performance" he'd seen in the games, and warned that the game makers, judges, and noble houses would be furious enough to end the party's tournament run—urging them to leave quickly before anyone could react. Source: [[2026.09.15|2026.09.15]].
