# Samantha Westermere

## Summary

Samantha Westermere is the ghost of a daughter of [[House Westermere]]. She is a floating, silvery woman whose hair drifts in an ethereal wind, haunting the undercroft of her family's home, [[Westermere Manor]]. She died in her own room there after *"many catastrophes"* led to her house's fall. She long refused to pass on until the living restore [[Havenport]] to how it was in her childhood. On [[2026.11.18]], with [[The Warden of Death]] destroyed and her family's spirits freed, she chose her own time: *"May the Mariner watch over you all. It is now my time to rest."* Source: [[2026.09.22|2026.09.22]], [[2026.11.18|2026.11.18]].

## Notes

- She emerged after [[Azrith]] leapt into the Curtain: *"Bold of your friend to take the gate."* She explained that it is *"a curtain to the realm of death"*, and that Azrith may return "if he survives" when it reopens, in a day or a week. She has seen groups go through it together.
- She showed the party the black veil, which is impenetrable to her. She said she learned *"more in the last five minutes than I have in the last five years"* from watching them touch it, and believes it is tied to the Curtain.
- Invited the party to stay in the manor's rooms, but warned that residents of "the other side" might try to possess them.
- Playful and a little cagey. She blushed at [[Cassian]]'s flirting, and he promised to return: *"A Fortescue always keeps their promise."*
- Doesn't know [[Ian]], the Oathkeeper upstairs.

Source: [[2026.09.22|2026.09.22]].

- Revealed that **[[House Wulfhelm]] killed her family** 15 years ago after her family learned a dire Wulfhelm secret. She and her family lie beyond the [[Black Veil|black veil]]. See [[Uncover the Westermere-Wulfhelm Coup]]. Source: [[2026.09.29|2026.09.29]].
- [[Odine Dunmere]] told [[Mauve Marigold]] that the party had spoken with Samantha again and that she sent her regards. Mauve called that very kind of her. No such conversation was shown in play. Source: [[2026.10.06|2026.10.06]].
- **On [[2026.11.18]], after the Warden's destruction, she drifted into the burial chamber** and knelt at the skeletal remains of her family — concealed until then beneath the mist — remembering them. Source: [[2026.11.18|2026.11.18]].
- She revealed **the Shepherd statue was a gift from [[House Wulfhelm]], intended as a trap**: *"House Wolfhelm gifted this statue to my family. It was intended as a trap."* Wulfhelm deliberately spread the Wither and twisted the divine warden into a magnet for souls. Source: [[2026.11.18|2026.11.18]].
- She sensed the **ancient magic within Odine** (*"You have an ancient magic that lives within you. I sense it."*) and warned the party: *"You must beware the ambitions of the noble houses. They do not understand the powers that they attempt to control."* Source: [[2026.11.18|2026.11.18]].
- Now that the monster is gone it *"will no longer attract the souls of the dead that die in the city"* — they are free to pass on. She said she would find her own way, spoke the parting blessing *"May the Mariner watch over you all"*, and **passed on to the great beyond**. Source: [[2026.11.18|2026.11.18]].
