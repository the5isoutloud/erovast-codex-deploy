# Eodwin Wulfhelm

## Summary

Eodwin Wulfhelm is a lord or prince of [[House Wulfhelm]] and the brother of Beowyn Wulfhelm. Together they learned something **dire about House Wulfhelm** — a secret they hoped to keep quiet. Eodwin's current wife had a child who, as a little girl, would visit the Westermeres in the summers. Eodwin knew the Westermere family well. Source: [[2026.09.29|2026.09.29]].

## Notes

- Eodwin and Beowyn learned a secret about House Wulfhelm that they hoped to keep quiet.
- The Westermere family discovered this secret.
- House Wulfhelm realized the Westermeres knew, and staged a trap that killed them all.
- Eodwin's wife's child (now grown) was a playmate of the Westermere daughters.
- The truth about what Eodwin and Beowyn discovered lies beyond the black veil in [[Westermere Manor]].
