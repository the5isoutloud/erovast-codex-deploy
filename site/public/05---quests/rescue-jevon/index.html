Rescue Jevon#

Status#

Partially resolved. Jevon was found in the Stone Sanctum graveyard, bound in rope and bloodied, and freed by Odine Dunmere. He is alive and recovering. However, he cannot safely return to the King's Tourney in daylight because House Godrin will be looking for him when they discover their two soldiers are missing. Source: 2026.05.05, 2026.05.12.

Evidence#

  • Azrith found Jevon’s magical box buried in muddy cart tracks after he was taken.
  • A small boy said Jevon dropped the box “when they took him” and identified the abductors as white-armored knights.
  • A scrap of green fabric torn on an iron candelabra in the Stone Sanctum matched Jevon’s cloak, confirming he had been brought there.
  • Jevon’s box flickered erratically as the party approached the graveyard, warning Odine his life force was in danger.
  • Jevon was found tied to a cherubim statue in the Stone Sanctum graveyard with a dead House Godrin soldier nearby.

What Happened#

House Godrin soldiers abducted Jevon and interrogated him about his magic and his teacher, Maralynn. He refused to give them much. They escalated and he passed out from the beating. One of the soldiers was killed by the possessing ghost before Jevon regained consciousness. The party defeated the second soldier (possessed by an undead spirit) in combat, after which the spirit submitted and dissipated. Source: 2026.05.12.

Current Status#

  • Jevon is in hiding, moving unseen through Everdale.
  • Odine Dunmere holds one of two enchanted stones Jevon made; she can send him one short message and receive a reply to her mind. She is to use it at night when the party has a secure meeting place.
  • Two dead House Godrin soldiers remain in the Stone Sanctum graveyard. When discovered, House Godrin will likely assume Jevon is responsible.
  • Jevon is willing to come to the party at night if called. Source: 2026.05.12.

Next Steps#

  • Contact Jevon at night via the communication stone once the party is settled at the Ragged Flagon.
  • Determine whether House Godrin’s search for Maralynn escalates after the deaths of their soldiers.
  • Prove to Gruvelda Duskbelt that the party handled the ghost problem at the Stone Sanctum (she requires proof, not just their word).

Ongoing Support (since 2026.06.23)#

Jevon visited the party’s tent in person on 2026.06.23, confirming House Godrin’s knights are still actively hunting him by day, and offered to gather intelligence from his cover as an evening armor/weapon repairman for tournament knights. Source: 2026.06.23.

In an untranscribed session before 2026.08.11, the party rescued Jevon a second time from House Godrin knights during “a bit of a ghost hunt”—no details survive beyond that. It was learned Godrin wants Jevon’s sending stone but, more importantly, wants to know who gave it to him—suggesting their real target is a third party, plausibly Maralynn. Source: 2026.08.11.

Jevon is now sheltering in the woods near the fairgrounds. The party set up a hidden dead-drop (a hollowed tree stump ~50 paces southeast of their tent) and has left him 50 gold and mead; he asked for coin to sustain himself and warned Odine that “they are powerful people.” Source: 2026.08.11.
