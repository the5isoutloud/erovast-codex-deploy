## Summary

White Feather Tor is a tall, craggy hill in the **Eastern Tourney Grounds**, topped with white tents, flags, and banners and reached by a well-trodden natural path. It is the **Godrin family camp**—[[House Godrin]]'s home base at the King's Tourney—and contains the **Messenger Tent**, the party's investigative target, as well as [[Golstang Godrin]]'s central pavilion. Source: [[2026.08.18|2026.08.18]].

## Notes

- The front approach is heavily guarded: 4–5 knights, closed gates, high walls—identified (Perception, natural 20 by [[Odine Dunmere]]) as the worst way in.
- A **sheer cliffside** on another face of the hill offers a dangerous but viable nighttime climb to infiltrate unseen.
- A vague "marsh-lock lake approach" was also mentioned as a more overt alternative way to seek entry—not explored.
- The **Messenger Tent**, somewhere on the tor, is named in [[Duke Tristan Blackwood]]'s intelligence note as a location tied to House Godrin's activity. Not yet entered.
- **Successfully infiltrated** on [[2026.08.25|2026.08.25]] by [[Cassian]] and [[Azrith]], both invisible (Cassian also flying). Azrith got in by shimmying up between two close-set gate pillars near the front gate. The Messenger Tent proved empty and locked down, but Azrith stole a guard's keyring and recovered an opened Godrin letter plus a strongbox with 73 gp and a potion of healing. Cassian eavesdropped on the main tent instead, overhearing [[Percival Godrin]] confirm [[Maralynn]] is alive. Cassian also spotted House Godrin's champion, **[[Sir Thorin Cassavar]] ("the Crow")**, patrolling the camp. Source: [[2026.08.25|2026.08.25]].

## Camp Layout (per Blackwood's Note)

- **Central Pavilion** — Golstang's seat; family business conducted here.
- **Family Tents** — Private quarters for the Godrin family.
- **Arcane Pavilion** — Warded and restricted.
- **Messenger Tent** — Correspondence and dispatches; the party's investigative target.
- **Training Grounds** — [[Ronan Godrin]] is frequently present here.
- **Supply Wagons** — The lightest-security area of the camp.

## Personnel (per Blackwood's Note)

- **~30 people total** within the camp.
- **6–8 knights** in distinctive matte-white Godrin armor.
- **12–15 men-at-arms** in white-and-grey Godrin livery.
- Servants, messengers, grooms, and arcane attendants.
- Security is **considerably higher after dark** along the perimeter—but once past the perimeter guards, there's considerably more freedom to move about inside.

## Notable Godrin Family Members (per Blackwood's Note)

- **[[Golstang Godrin]]** — House patron. Stationed at the central pavilion. Heavily guarded.
- **[[Percival Godrin]]** — Frequently moves between the pavilion, Everdale, and the tourney grounds.
- **[[Emberlee Godrin]]** — Keeps mostly to the Godrin encampment. Rarely alone.
- **[[Valerie Godrin]]** — Regularly leaves camp. The most accessible Godrin away from the family.
- **[[Ronan Godrin]]** — Frequently found around the competitor and training grounds.

## Relationships

- [[House Godrin]]: This is their family camp; the tor and its Messenger Tent are tied to their activity.
- [[Duke Tristan Blackwood]]: Provided the intelligence note detailing this camp's layout and personnel.
- [[Investigate House Godrin]]: The tor is the target of the party's planned nighttime infiltration.
