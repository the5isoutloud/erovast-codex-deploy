## Summary

Edith is the bartender and server at the [[Salt House]] in [[Havenport]]. She serves the party their meals and drinks, handles room bookings, and provides local information. Source: [[2026.09.29|2026.09.29]].

## Notes

- She runs the bar at the Salt House, often not paying attention to customers until addressed.
- Mauve defers to her for serving: "Edith, we have visitors. Make them comfortable, please."
- She brings the party's food and drinks.
- She handles room bookings: common room cot (1 silver/night), private room (3 silver/night, only one available).
- She seems to have a romantic interest in [[Ian]]: when Cassian says Ian is charming, she says "he doesn't come to visit, I sure as hell ain't going up there."
- She takes pride in the food and drinks, smiling humbly when Cassian tips generously.

## Relationships

- [[Salt House]]: Works here as bartender and server.
- [[Mauve Marigold]]: Works under Mauve's direction.
- [[Ian]]: She has a romantic interest in him, but he doesn't visit the Salt House.
