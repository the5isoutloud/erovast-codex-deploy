# Everdale

## Summary

Everdale is the human capital city and the host of the annual [[King's Tourney]]. Roads converge here toward a hill crowned by the [[Gleaming Keep]], with the tournament grounds and [[King's Tourney]] spreading through the valley below. Source: [[2026.05.05|2026.05.05]].

## Notable Features

- **[[Gleaming Keep]]:** a keep built from stone supposedly transmuted into metal by King Aldrich's mages. It sits on a cliff above the city and reflects the light, giving it its name. Source: [[2026.05.05|2026.05.05]].
- **[[Empty Throne]]:** the throne once held by King Aldrich. It has not been filled for centuries. Source: [[2026.05.05|2026.05.05]].
- **[[King's Tourney]]:** a two-week annual tournament that brings Everdale to life and sustains much of the local economy. Source: [[2026.05.05|2026.05.05]].
- **[[Stone Sanctum]]:** an old moss-and-vine-covered cathedral in the hamlet above the tourney grounds, with an attached graveyard. Source: [[2026.05.12|2026.05.12]].
- **[[Ragged Flagon]]:** a makeshift tavern in the King's Tourney campgrounds where nobles don't typically frequent. Source: [[2026.05.12|2026.05.12]].
- **[[Boarhead Tor]]:** the elevated noble camp hill where [[House Wulfhelm]] is based. Source: [[2026.05.19|2026.05.19]].
- **[[Clover Hill]]:** the noble encampment above the fairgrounds, where [[Duke Tristan Blackwood]] keeps his tent. Source: [[2026.06.11|2026.06.11]].
- **[[Crown Gauntlet]]:** the Proving Grounds' 40-foot obstacle tower, which the party cleared on [[2026.07.14|2026.07.14]].
- **[[The Menagerie]]:** the beast-arena tier of the tourney, where the party freed a captive manticore. Source: [[2026.08.11|2026.08.11]], [[2026.09.08|2026.09.08]].
- **[[White Feather Tor]]:** [[House Godrin]]'s family camp in the Eastern Tourney Grounds, which the party infiltrated on [[2026.08.25|2026.08.25]].

## Governance

Everdale is protected and administered by the [[Oathkeepers]], an order formed from three knightly orders. They protect the keep, guard the Empty Throne, police the tournament, and help oversee the local economy and settlement. They believe King Aldrich will someday return. Source: [[2026.05.05|2026.05.05]].

## Noble Houses

- [[House Godrin]]: a wealthy noble house known for mana crystals, artifacts, arcane power, and a ruthless belief in the right to rule. [[Cassian]] recognized their white armor and banner at the tourney grounds. Source: [[2026.05.05|2026.05.05]].
- [[House Wulfhelm]]: one of the most powerful houses in the Crownlands. They won last year's King's Tourney and currently hold the title and deed to the [[Thornwood Vale]]. Source: [[2026.05.05|2026.05.05]].

## Party Visit

[[Jevon]] guided [[Odine Dunmere]], [[Cassian]], [[Aldrich]], and [[Azrith]] into the city for the King's Tourney, then disappeared into the crowd. The party registered for the Proving Grounds as **Cassian's Crushing Crusaders**. They later discovered Jevon had been taken by [[House Godrin]] soldiers and found him tied up in the [[Stone Sanctum]] graveyard. After rescuing him and defeating a possessed knight, the party learned that House Godrin was interrogating Jevon about his teacher, a witch named [[Maralynn]] from the [[Thornwood Vale]]. See [[Rescue Jevon]] and [[Investigate the Pestilence]]. Source: [[2026.05.05|2026.05.05]], [[2026.05.12|2026.05.12]].
