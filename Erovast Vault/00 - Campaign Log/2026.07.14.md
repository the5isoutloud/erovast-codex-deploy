# 2026.07.14

## Summary

This session was almost entirely the continuation and conclusion of the **[[Crown Gauntlet]]** from [[2026.06.23|2026.06.23]], picking back up at countdown 8, Cheer Marks 7/10, Mud Marks 2/10. Source: [[2026.07.14 - Transcript|Transcript]].

The party crossed the spinning logs (Odine 22, Azrith 15 while maintaining **Bless**, Aldrich via a running jump). **[[Odine Dunmere]]** cast **Mold Earth** to bury the mud-stuck guard to the waist, effectively restraining him for the rest of the fight. **[[Cassian]]** flew to the platform and used **Thaumaturgy** for a booming "Are you not entertained?"—a Performance roll of 19 (+4) that maxed the crowd's **Cheer Marks at 10/10**, granting the whole party (barring Azrith, who couldn't benefit from his own Bless) a permanent stacking **+1d4** on all remaining Gauntlet rolls. Source: [[2026.07.14 - Transcript|Transcript]].

At the sheer plank wall, **[[Azrith]]** rolled a natural 1 followed by a natural 20 (with advantage) on Investigation, coaching the team and dropping the climb DC from 18 to 15 for everyone. [[Aldrich]] still needed his one Heroic Inspiration reroll to clear it; Odine barely made it, caught by Cassian's grip. Beyond the wall, gear-driven platforms rotated around a central tower, guarded by a knight throwing heavy sacks from above and another who jammed his jousting stick into the gears to freeze the mechanism. Azrith's improvised intimidation with his **Ring of Fire Breath** (natural 1) failed to scare the guard loose but still applied 6 fire damage; Aldrich's bluffed "magic" threat (rolled a 1) and a missed javelin throw both failed. Cassian ultimately flew down and used the mystery **chain**—given to Aldrich mid-combat the prior session, its exact properties still unidentified by the table—to yank the guard's stick free with an advantage-boosted Strength check, freeing the platforms. Source: [[2026.07.14 - Transcript|Transcript]].

Odine's **Gust** cantrip forced the top guard back 5 feet (failed to knock him off); her deliberately low-hovering **Fog Cloud** blinded the pursuing ground guards without blocking the party's own elevated sightlines. Aldrich took a natural-20 sack throw for 8 bludgeoning, nearly fell, and clawed back to the ledge. Cassian then engaged the top guard directly: a Thaumaturgy-flame-plus-perfume-bottle "flamethrower" (hit, but rolled a 1 on damage), a **Taunting Step** teleport that forced the guard into disadvantage against anyone but Cassian, a dodged grapple, and finally a contested shove (guard rolled a natural 2) that knocked him to a lower platform for 2d6 fall damage. Cassian draped the chain over the ledge for Aldrich and Azrith to climb up. Source: [[2026.07.14 - Transcript|Transcript]].

With the guard back on his feet, **Odine** landed an **Eldritch Blast** (Repelling Blast) for 9 force damage, knocking him 10 feet off the platform entirely—the fall left him incapacitated (Odine is multiclassed Warlock 2 / Sorcerer 2, so Eldritch Blast is one of her own cantrips). With the countdown at its final round, Azrith lowered Cassian's chain to Odine as her platform rotated out from under her; Aldrich's "Brutus drag" (14) hauled her partway, and Cassian's assisted pull (15 vs. a lowered DC 10) finished the rescue. **All four party members reached the top platform together and rang the bell with six seconds left on the clock—the Crown Gauntlet is complete.** Source: [[2026.07.14 - Transcript|Transcript]].

The crowd erupted. **[[Duke Tristan Blackwood]]**, watching from the sponsor's box, gave only a slow, reserved clap—"amused and amazed" rather than jubilant. In the victory roleplay: Cassian rose above the bell tower and delivered a bombastic speech naming **[[Rory]]** by name as a loser of the crowd's bets (Rory is shown in the crowd throwing his hat in the mud); Azrith taunted the crowd with his scimitar drawn; Odine, feeling guilty for having blasted the final guard twice, cast **Healing Word** on him; Aldrich asked to retrieve his lost javelin from the mud. The GM closed the session here, promising to pick up next time with "the after-effects of winning." Source: [[2026.07.14 - Transcript|Transcript]].
