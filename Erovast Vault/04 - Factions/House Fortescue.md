# House Fortescue

## Summary

House Fortescue is **[[Cassian]]'s family house**, of celestial or semi-divine bloodline—descended from a lineage cut off from the divine but still carrying celestial heritage. Confirmed as a **vassal of [[House Wulfhelm]]** for roughly 60 years (established ~40 years after King Aldrich's reign ended, around a century ago). Source: [[2026.06.11|2026.06.11]], [[2026.08.18|2026.08.18]].

## Notes

- [[Duke Tristan Blackwood]], despite "running in a lot of circles," has never once heard House Wulfhelm acknowledge or claim House Fortescue as a vassal—and finds this odd enough to remark on directly. Source: [[2026.08.18|2026.08.18]].
- Cassian deflected the question rather than explain: *"There are few who remember us fondly... that is part of the reason I am here."* This implies some falling-out, disgrace, or forgotten history attached to the house that Cassian has not yet revealed to the party or the wiki. Source: [[2026.08.18|2026.08.18]].
- Cassian's family signet ring (a falling star meeting its own tail, ouroboros-like) marks the house as old-blooded nobility. See [[Cassian]].
- Blackwood did not consider the Fortescue name "risky" to associate with, despite the discrepancy.
- The old [[Oathkeepers|Oathkeeper]] [[Ian]] in [[Havenport]] recognized the name at once and called [[Cassian]] *"Lord Fortescue's son"*: *"people tend to have short memories, don't they."* Source: [[2026.09.22|2026.09.22]].

## Relationships

- [[Cassian]]: Scion of the house.
- [[House Wulfhelm]]: Nominal liege house—though Wulfhelm apparently does not acknowledge the vassalage publicly.
- [[Duke Tristan Blackwood]]: Suspicious of the unacknowledged vassalage; pressed Cassian on it directly.
