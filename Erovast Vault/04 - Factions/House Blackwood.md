# House Blackwood

## Summary

House Blackwood is a lower-tier vassal house at the [[King's Tourney]]. They hold a smaller parcel of land loaned to them by one of the greater houses and are not one of the big three noble houses. They do not typically win tournaments. Their representative at the current tournament is **[[Duke Tristan Blackwood]]**. Source: [[00 - Game Log/2026.06.02|2026.06.02]].

## Notes

- [[Duke Tristan Blackwood]] watched the party's first Proving Grounds bout from the noble box and made eye contact with [[Cassian]] afterward, signaling interest.
- On a similar level of nobility to [[Cassian]]'s family vassal house under [[House Wulfhelm]].
- House Blackwood is a **vassal of [[House Corwyn]]**, one of the three dominant houses at the tournament. Duke Blackwood operates within Corwyn's sphere of influence and reports up to them. Source: [[00 - Game Log/2026.06.11|2026.06.11]].
- Duke Blackwood formalized a **sponsorship deal with the party at 8%** of prize money, providing a 750 gp advance and four combat items. Source: [[00 - Game Log/2026.06.11|2026.06.11]].

## Relationships

- [[Duke Tristan Blackwood]]: Their midlord representative at the King's Tourney.
- [[House Corwyn]]: Their liege house; House Blackwood is a vassal of Corwyn.
- [[King's Tourney]]: Competing house with modest standing; now sponsors Cassian's Crushing Crusaders.
