# Ragged Flagon

## Summary

The Ragged Flagon is a makeshift tavern set up in the campgrounds of the [[King's Tourney]] in [[Everdale]]. It caters to commoners and those who prefer to stay out of the notice of noble houses. [[Jevon]] recommended it to the party as a good place to lie low, noting that the nobles tend not to frequent it. Source: [[2026.05.12|2026.05.12]].

## Notes

- Recommended by [[Jevon]] after the fight at the [[Stone Sanctum]] as a safe lodging option.
- Run by [[Rory]], an orc bartender. House ale is 1 silver per flagon—cheap, gut-rumbling, and effective.
- [[Gruvelda Duskbelt]] was spotted here by [[Azrith]] (natural 20 perception) the party's first night, hiding under a hood in a corner booth and keeping a very low profile.
- The Ragged Flagon is tent-only—no rooms to let. Participants with tourney registration have access to the free open campgrounds nearby.
- The party has since bought their own tent and campsite nearby (funded by [[Duke Tristan Blackwood]]'s sponsorship money), making this area their base of operations. Source: [[2026.08.11|2026.08.11]].
- [[Rory]] has now lost two bets against the party (their first Proving Grounds bout, then the [[Crown Gauntlet]]); [[Aldrich]] also stole an entire barrel of his mead undetected. Source: [[2026.06.23|2026.06.23]], [[2026.08.11|2026.08.11]].
